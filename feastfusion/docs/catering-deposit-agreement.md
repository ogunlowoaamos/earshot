## 4. THE DEPOSIT IS NON-REFUNDABLE

**4.1** The booking deposit is **non-refundable in every circumstance**, with the single exception in clause 8 (where FeastFusion cancels).

**4.2** The deposit is a **commitment fee**, not payment for work already performed. By accepting it, FeastFusion:

- (a) holds your date exclusively and turns away other enquiries for that date;
- (b) begins purchasing ingredients, booking staff and committing funds to your event;
- (c) loses the ability to earn from that date if you withdraw.

**4.3** If you cancel — for **any** reason, including but not limited to change of mind, change of date, venue problems, a reduced guest count, illness, bereavement, weather, travel difficulty, or the event simply not holding — **the deposit is retained by FeastFusion in full.** No part of it is returned.

**4.4** If the event proceeds as agreed, the deposit is credited **in full** against your final invoice. Where the event goes ahead, the deposit is not money lost.

**4.5** The deposit is not transferable to another person or another event, except under clause 7.

## 5. Balance Payment

**5.1** The balance is payable no later than **7 days before the event**, unless a different date is written here: ______________________.

**5.2** Final guest numbers must be confirmed at the same time as the balance.

**5.3** If the balance is not paid by the due date, FeastFusion may treat the booking as cancelled by you. Clause 4 then applies and the deposit is retained.

**5.4** Payments are by bank transfer or the online payment link only. Please keep your receipt.

---

## 6. Changes to Guest Numbers

**6.1 Increases** are accepted up to **7 days** before the event, subject to kitchen capacity, at the agreed per-head rate.

**6.2 Decreases** made **7 days or more** before the event reduce the price to the new confirmed count.

**6.3 Decreases** made **within 7 days** of the event **do not reduce the price.** You pay for the higher of the confirmed count and the final count, because ingredients and staff are already committed.

**6.4** FeastFusion will not serve more guests than the confirmed and paid-for count.

---

## 7. Rescheduling

**7.1** You may reschedule **once**, free of charge, if you give written notice **at least 21 days** before the event, and only:

- (a) to a date FeastFusion can still serve; and
- (b) within **6 months** of the original date.

Your deposit carries over to the new date.

**7.2** A **second** reschedule is treated as a cancellation. Clause 4 applies.

**7.3** Notice given **less than 21 days** before the event is treated as a cancellation. Clause 4 applies.

---

## 8. If FeastFusion Cancels

**8.1** If FeastFusion cancels for any reason within our control, you may choose either:

- (a) a **full refund** of the deposit within **14 days**; or
- (b) the deposit carried over to a new date we both agree.

**8.2** If FeastFusion cannot perform because of an event outside our control (fire, flood, civil unrest, government restriction, sudden serious illness of key staff, or a similar event), you may choose either:

- (a) the deposit carried over to a new date we both agree; or
- (b) a **full refund**, less only amounts already paid to third parties specifically for your event, which FeastFusion will evidence with receipts.

**8.3** Clause 8 is the **only** situation in which any part of the deposit is returned.

## 9. Menu and Dietary Requirements

**9.1** The final menu is locked **14 days** before the event. After that, no substitutions.

**9.2** All allergies, intolerances and dietary requirements must be given to FeastFusion **in writing** at least 14 days before the event.

**9.3** FeastFusion prepares food in a kitchen that also handles peanuts, tree nuts, shellfish, fish, eggs, milk, wheat and soya. **We cannot guarantee an allergen-free dish.**

## 11. Food Safety and Leftovers

**11.1** Food is served at safe temperatures. Once served, food that sits out for **more than 2 hours** is no longer safe to eat and FeastFusion accepts no responsibility for it.

**11.2** Leftovers are only packed if you supply containers, and only if requested before service.

**11.3** FeastFusion will not release food to guests before the agreed service time.
